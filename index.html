WHERE HAVE I SPENT MY LIFE?

A life is distributed across cities, countries and continents: some places take up years, others only a moment. Duration, however, is not the same as intensity. A few days can sometimes matter more than years. This map traces that distribution through the places I’ve lived, studied, worked and travelled, from a few days to several years.
